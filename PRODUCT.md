# Vibe App

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

College students looking for spontaneous hangouts and a crew. Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-26.

## Product Purpose

Help students discover, create, and join plans, then coordinate with the people attending.

## Operating Context

Existing React and Vite application with Supabase authentication and data. Discovery supports activity and proximity filters. Students can manage hosted and joined plans, requests, itineraries, group chat, friends, notifications, profiles, and host ratings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing data, authentication, permissions, workflows, and local changes. Refresh every existing screen and modal, supporting mobile and desktop. Backend operations are outside the design scope.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Vibe App. The user requested a youthful design for all pages. No fixed palette or typeface was supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

Current components and data structures in src/. No testimonials, adoption metrics, or marketing claims are provided.

## Product Principles

- Make finding a hangout and understanding its practical details easy.
- Give joining and hosting equal attention.
- Keep the social experience friendly and usable on small screens.
